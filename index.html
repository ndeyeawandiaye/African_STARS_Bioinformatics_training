From FASTQ to Outbreak Insights

Genomic surveillance training — African STARS Fellowship · CARE · Institut Pasteur

The learning path

# Module You will learn to…
1 FASTQ → consensus genome read FASTQ files, do QC, map reads, inspect coverage, build a consensus and decide if it is usable
2 Alignment, lineage & reference selection choose a reference, align genomes, assign a lineage
3 Build and read a phylogenetic tree infer a tree, root it, read clades, support and branch lengths
4 Communicating genomic evidence turn results into clear, honest statements for decision makers
5 Mini outbreak investigation put everything together on a three-region outbreak

Module 1 is the 3-hour bridge session (talk + dry lab); modules 2–3 are Day 4 (phylogenetics) and modules 4–5 are Day 5 (capstone).

How to work

  1. Do the setup once.
  2. Follow the pages in the order of the left menu; each exercise ends with a question to discuss.
  3. Fill in your answer sheet and, on Day 5, your one-page brief.
